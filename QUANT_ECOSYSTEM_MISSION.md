# Quant Ecosystem 3.0 — Autonomous Alpha Platform
## Vision, Boundaries, Priorities & Activation Roadmap

---

## 1. TRUE VISION

This project aims to build a lean institutional-grade autonomous trading research organism.

The system must:

- Discover trading strategies automatically
- Evaluate and evolve strategies continuously
- Allocate capital dynamically
- Adapt to market regimes
- Learn from real trading outcomes
- Survive extreme market conditions
- Improve performance over time

This is NOT:

- A signal generator
- A single strategy bot
- A static backtest framework
- A UI-first trading product
- An endless research sandbox

This IS:

> A continuously learning intraday alpha research + execution organism.

---

## 2. TARGET CAPABILITY (REALISTIC)

When stabilized, the system should be able to:

- Run autonomous research cycles daily
- Generate new alpha candidates
- Promote only high-quality alphas
- Maintain a live alpha portfolio
- Execute trades in controlled size
- Track real PnL and survival statistics
- Adapt discovery behavior to regime changes
- Improve allocation decisions over weeks/months

The goal is NOT perfect prediction.

The goal is:

> Sustainable adaptive edge.

---

## 3. CORE PRINCIPLES

### 3.1 Reality First

Market feedback is the ultimate teacher.

No architecture decision is complete until validated in:
- paper trading
- micro live trading
- drawdown observation

### 3.2 Avoid Infinite Engineering

No new major subsystem should be added if:
- execution loop not stabilized
- portfolio assimilation not working
- real PnL logs not available

### 3.3 Lean Institutional Design

System should resemble institutional thinking but remain:

- lightweight
- debuggable
- observable
- capital efficient

### 3.4 Survival > Aggression

System must prioritize:
- risk containment
- regime awareness
- capital preservation

before profit maximization.

"Building more intelligence"

"Making intelligence act in market."

---

## 6. IMMEDIATE PRIORITIES (STRICT ORDER)

### Priority 1 — Portfolio Assimilation

- Promoted alphas must enter alpha_book
- Exposure limits must be enforced
- Allocation must be deterministic

### Priority 2 — Paper Trading Activation

- Signals must route through execution bridge
- Simulated fills must generate PnL
- PnL must update lifecycle and evolution memory

### Priority 3 — Observability

System must log:

- alpha birth / death
- portfolio size
- trade count
- win rate
- drawdown
- regime transitions

### Priority 4 — Weekly Evolution Cycle

Example structure:

- Monday–Thursday → research + paper trading
- Tuesday → micro live trading (controlled size)
- Weekend → parameter tuning / pruning

---

## 7. WHAT MUST BE AVOIDED

- Adding new intelligence engines prematurely
- Expanding symbol universe too early
- Increasing parallel load before stability
- Chasing architectural perfection
- Emotional reaction to short-term performance
- Full capital deployment before survival metrics

---

## 8. SUCCESS METRICS

Early Success:

- System runs daily without crash
- Generates tradeable signals
- Maintains controlled exposure
- Logs usable performance data

Intermediate Success:

- Alpha survival improves over weeks
- Drawdown stabilizes
- Capital curve shows structure

Advanced Success:

- Discovery adapts to regime shifts
- Portfolio composition evolves autonomously
- Yield becomes consistent (not necessarily high)

---

## 9. ROLE OF FUTURE CONTRIBUTORS / AI / CHATS

Anyone entering this project must:

- Respect activation priorities
- Avoid introducing unrelated features
- Focus on making existing subsystems operational
- Improve stability before intelligence expansion
- Tie every change to real market behavior

---

## 10. FINAL OBJECTIVE

To operate a semi-autonomous intraday quant organism that:

- learns continuously
- allocates capital rationally
- survives adverse regimes
- compounds edge gradually

This is a long-term adaptive system, not a short-term profit hack.
